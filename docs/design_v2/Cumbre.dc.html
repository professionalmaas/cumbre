Top 3 fixes

Do these in order. Each one is a real step up the wall.

1

Lift the second chorus

+6 Music

The summit chorus lands at the same height as the first. Stack a harmony or octave vocal so the peak actually feels higher.

2

Anchor verse 2's imagery

+5 Lyrics

Lines 3–4 drift abstract. Tie them to one concrete image and the story pulls climbers all the way in.

3

Carve space in the low-mids

+4 Mix

200–400 Hz is crowded. Trim the rhythm guitar a couple dB so the vocal can breathe on the climb.
